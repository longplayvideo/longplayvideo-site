About

Long Play Video is a film podcast hosted by Kev (Sydney) and Andy (UK) - two mates working through their top 100 favourite movies of all time, one film a week, from opposite sides of the planet.

Season 1: The Video Shop Era

It started behind the counter of a nostalgic, Blockbuster-style video rental shop, working through Andy's top 100 favourite films of all time.

Season 2: Cocktails and Shattered Dreams

Now it's a tiki bar on a hidden beach island, tropical drinks in hand, working through Kev's top 100 favourite films of all time.
